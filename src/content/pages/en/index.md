# Operating Systems Lab

THU-OSLAB is the Operating Systems Lab in the [Department of Computer Science and Technology](https://www.cs.tsinghua.edu.cn/) at [Tsinghua University](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/). We design and study system software for emerging hardware and workloads, with performance, reliability, and security as central concerns.

## Research areas

- **Kernels and architecture**: kernel components, multicore scalability, and operating systems built with Rust and RISC-V.
- **System security and reliability**: vulnerability analysis, trusted execution, verification, and secure virtualization.
- **AI and operating systems**: resource scheduling and memory management for AI workloads, alongside AI-assisted system design.
